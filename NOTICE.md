result data. SKY130, ngspice, OpenVAF, IEEEtran and cited publications retain
their own licenses. Their runtime binaries, PDK source and publication files are

module scope documented in their source. The frozen source snapshot preserves
its historical imports and file names; use the supported reproduction command
in README.md rather than attempting to execute every historical module.
